Our Story

A different kind of club.
Your in between place between home and work.

The Courthouse was created around a simple belief:
Sport should bring people together.

Not just for an hour on court, but before the game, after the game, and long after the score has been forgotten.

We wanted to create something Bratislava was missing — a place built around the energy of sport and the culture of a great social club.

A place to compete.

A place to connect.

A place to belong.

Welcome to The Courthouse.

The Idea

More than courts.
More than a sport arena.

The Courthouse began with pickleball, but it was never meant to end there.

We imagined a club where a morning match turns into coffee. Where strangers become doubles partners. Where competition lives comfortably alongside conversation. Where you might arrive for an hour and find yourself staying for three.

Seven indoor courts form the heart of the club. Around them, we’ve created everything that makes you want to stay — a coffee bar, Pro Shop, social spaces, coaching, tournaments and a calendar of experiences that goes beyond traditional sport.

The court brings us together. What happens around it makes us a club.

Our Philosophy

Exclusive in experience.
Inclusive by nature.

We believe a club can feel special without making people feel excluded.

The Courthouse is built with the attention to detail, atmosphere and standards you’d expect from a private members’ club — but you don’t need the right surname, the right connections or years on a waiting list to walk through our doors.

Never held a racket? You’re welcome.

Playing your hundredth tournament? You’re welcome.

Just Chilling? You’re welcome.

Come alone. Come with friends. Come to compete. Come because you’re curious.

The only thing you need to belong here is a desire to be part of it.

The Social Club

Come for the game.
Stay for the club.

Some of the best moments at The Courthouse won’t have a score.

They’ll happen over coffee after a match. Watching friends from the sidelines. Meeting your next doubles partner. Staying longer than you planned.

And sometimes we’ll deliberately blur the line between sport and social life.

The Game

Competition belongs here.
So does your first game.

Pickleball is easy to start and difficult to stop playing.

That’s part of what drew us to it. Pickleball has captured hearts all over US and Asia, now it is time for Europe. Join the biggest growing sport globally.

It can be competitive without being intimidating, social without losing its sporting edge, and played across generations and ability levels.

At The Courthouse, beginners can discover the game alongside experienced players, coaches and competitors. Badminton has a home here too, giving our courts and community another way to play.

Learn the game. Find your level. Find your people. Then see how far you want to take it.

The Community

Beautiful courts don’t create a community.

People do. A club is defined by its people and their stories.

The Courthouse is designed to make meeting them easier — through open play, leagues, coaching, tournaments, club events and the simple possibility of sitting down for a coffee after your match.

Different ages. Different backgrounds. Different levels of play.

One court is often all it takes to put everyone on equal ground.

You don’t have to know anyone when you arrive.
We hope you will when you leave.

Membership

For those who make it their club.

You don’t need to be a member to belong at The Courthouse.

Membership is simply our way of giving something more to the people who make the club part of their routine.

More play. More access. Member advantages. Priority opportunities. Special experiences. And a closer connection to what’s happening inside the club.

Open to everyone. Made special for those who keep coming back. Your second home.
