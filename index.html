Our Story

More than a place to play.

Redefining Bratislava scene and becoming a social hub within the vibrant community.

The Courthouse started with a simple idea: Sport is better when it brings people together.

We created a place where you can compete, learn, meet people, stay for coffee, and become part of a community — whether you’re picking up a racket for the first time or playing every week. Your much needed place in between home and work.

Come for the game. Stay for everything around it.
