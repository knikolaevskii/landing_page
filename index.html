Our Story

More than a place to play.

The Courthouse started with a simple idea: sport is better when it brings people together.

A place where you can compete, learn, meet people, stay for coffee and become part of a community — whether you're picking up a racket for the first time or playing every week.

Come for the game. Stay for everything around it.
