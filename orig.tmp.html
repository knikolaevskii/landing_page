Questions

Membership — FAQs

The things people ask before they join. If your question is not here, ask us directly.

Do I have to be a member to play at The Court House?

No. Anyone can book a court at the standard rate — 27 € per hour off-peak, 33 € at peak, with racket rental at 5 €. Membership lowers those rates, opens the booking calendar earlier and moves you up the queue for club events.

When are peak and off-peak hours?

Peak covers [weekday evenings 17:00–21:00 and weekend mornings]. Everything outside that is off-peak. The price you see when booking already reflects which one applies.

Can I switch plans after signing up?

Yes — you can move between tiers at any time, and the new rate applies from your next billing month.

How do I cancel my membership?

Membership runs month to month and can be cancelled at any time; it stays active until the end of the month you have already paid for.] Write to us through the contact form and we will take care of it.
