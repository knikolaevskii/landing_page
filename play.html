New to Pickleball?

You'll get it in minutes.

Never played? Perfect. Pickleball combines elements of tennis, badminton and table tennis into a game that's remarkably easy to pick up.

You need a paddle, a ball, a court and someone on the other side of the net. We'll help with the rest.

The basics

  • FormatSingles or doubles — doubles is by far the most popular.
  • ServeServe diagonally, into the service court across the net.
  • Two bouncesLet the ball bounce once on each side before you start volleying.
  • The kitchenDon't volley while standing in the zone closest to the net.
  • ScoringPlay your way to 11, win by two.

That's enough to get your first game started. And yes, we'll explain the rest when you arrive.

No equipment? No problem. Paddles and balls are available at the club.
