Pickleball

Easy to start. Hard to put down.

There’s a reason pickleball is one of the fastest-growing sports in the world.

It’s quick to learn, immediately social and competitive enough to keep you coming back. You don’t need years of experience to have a great game but there’s always another level to reach.

The Courthouse is built around it.

Seven dedicated indoor courts give you a place to play year-round, whether you’re here for a casual game between friends, training for competition or somewhere in between.

Come with your own group or come looking for one.

This is your court.

Coaching

Get better at the game.

Your first game gets you interested.

Getting better keeps you coming back.

Work with experienced coaches through individual lessons, group training and clinics designed for different levels of play.

Start with the fundamentals. Improve your movement. Understand positioning. Develop your shots. Learn the strategy behind the game.

Or simply figure out why everyone keeps telling you to stop standing in the kitchen.

Choose how you learn

  • Private CoachingOne-to-one attention built around your game.
  • Group CoachingLearn together, improve together.
  • Beginner SessionsEverything you need to go from “What is pickleball?” to playing independently.
  • Clinics & MasterclassesFocused sessions for players looking to sharpen particular parts of their game.
